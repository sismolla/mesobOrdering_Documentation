MesobOrdering is a cloud-based hotel ordering platform built for hospitality teams that want to eliminate paper menus, reduce service bottlenecks, and deliver a seamless dining experience. Whether you run a boutique hotel restaurant or a large multi-department property, MesobOrdering gives your guests a polished digital menu they can access instantly by scanning a QR code — no app download required. Hotel admins and hospitality managers get a single dashboard to control every aspect of the operation, from menu content and staff accounts to real-time order tracking and revenue analytics.

## How It Works

MesobOrdering keeps the guest experience simple and the back-of-house experience powerful. The entire ordering flow runs in three steps:

1. **Guest scans the QR code** placed on their table. Their device opens the digital menu instantly in a browser — no login, no app install.
2. **Guest browses the menu and places an order.** They can explore categories, view item descriptions and prices, and submit their order with a single tap.
3. **Admin and staff manage everything from the dashboard.** Orders flow in real time to the relevant department — kitchen, bar, or cashier — while the admin monitors operations, updates menus, and reviews analytics from one central panel.

## System Roles

MesobOrdering uses a role-based access model so every team member sees exactly what they need. The five roles are designed around the real responsibilities of hotel staff.

| Role | What They Can Do |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Full access to all settings, menus, staff accounts, analytics, pricing, loyalty programs, and fraud controls. The primary account for hotel managers. |
| **Waiter** | Views incoming orders assigned to their tables, updates order status, and coordinates with kitchen and bar staff during service. |
| **Kitchen** | Receives food orders in real time via the Kitchen View, marks items as in-progress or completed, and manages preparation queues. |
| **Bar** | Receives drink orders in real time, manages beverage preparation status, and coordinates with waiters for delivery. |
| **Cashier** | Views completed orders, processes payments, applies discounts or loyalty rewards, and closes out guest tabs. |
