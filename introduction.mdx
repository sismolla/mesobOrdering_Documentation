**Turn tables faster. Keep guests happier. Control inventory with audit precision. All with a scan.**

MesobOrdering is a next-generation, cloud-based **Hospitality Operating System & Digital Ordering Platform**. It replaces static paper menus with dynamic, interactive QR codes. It connects guest-side ordering with staff-side operations — kitchen, bar, storekeeper, head chef, waiter, and cashier — in real time, backed by an Odoo-style multi-location append-only inventory engine.

## System Roles

MesobOrdering uses a role-based access model so every team member sees exactly what they need.

| Role | What They Can Do |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Full access to all settings, menus, inventory, staff accounts, analytics, pricing, loyalty, and audit controls. |
| **Storekeeper** | Receives physical goods (GRN), issues POs, manages packaging rules, dispatches transfers, and performs warehouse audits. |
| **Head Chef** | Executes kitchen prep batches (Injera, butchery, kibbeh), manages recipes, requests transfers, and monitors food stock. |
| **Waiter** | Views active table sessions, takes orders, and coordinates delivery. |
| **Kitchen** | Receives food orders in real time via KDS display, updating status from preparing to ready. |
| **Bar** | Receives drink orders in real time via Bar display, updating beverage fulfillment status. |
| **Cashier** | Processes bills, verifies TeleBirr/CBE payments, reconciles shift cash floats, and awards loyalty stamps. |
