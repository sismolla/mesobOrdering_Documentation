Owners get full audit transparency over every action.

The best part? It costs **completely zero** to get started.

## How It Works

MesobOrdering runs end-to-end in five frictionless steps — from the moment a guest sits down to the moment the table is cleared.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan">
    The guest scans a **table-specific QR code**. The menu opens instantly in their mobile browser — no app download, no Wi-Fi login required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order">
    They browse a beautiful, localized menu, customize items ("no onions", "extra spicy"), and submit their order — one tap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route">
    The system **splits the order automatically**. Food items route to the Kitchen Display; drinks route to the Bar Display. No paper tickets, no shouting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Serve">
    Waiters track live session times on the **Waiter Dashboard** and hear an audio alert the moment a guest taps the "Call Waiter" button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Guests request the bill from their phone. Cashiers generate an itemized, tax-calculated receipt with **TeleBirr, CBE Birr, or cash**. One confirmation clears the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## System Roles

MesobOrdering uses a role-based access model so every team member sees exactly what they need. The five roles are designed around the real responsibilities of hotel staff.

| Role | What They Can Do |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Full access to all settings, menus, staff accounts, analytics, pricing, loyalty programs, and fraud controls. The primary account for hotel managers. |
| **Waiter** | Views incoming orders assigned to their tables, updates order status, and coordinates with kitchen and bar staff during service. |
| **Kitchen** | Receives food orders in real time via the Kitchen View, marks items as in-progress or completed, and manages preparation queues. |
| **Bar** | Receives drink orders in real time, manages beverage preparation status, and coordinates with waiters for delivery. |
| **Cashier** | Views completed orders, processes payments, applies discounts or loyalty rewards, and closes out guest tabs. |
