## No Hardware Lock-In

Other systems force you to buy proprietary tablets and printers. MesobOrdering runs on the hardware you already own:

- **Kitchen displays** — cheap Android TVs, old iPads, or any web browser.
- **Bar displays** — same as kitchen. Any screen with a browser works.
- **Waiter devices** — staff smartphones. Nothing to buy.
- **Cashier station** — any laptop, desktop, or POS monitor.

The only physical items you need are **printed QR cards for your tables**, which you can print yourself.
