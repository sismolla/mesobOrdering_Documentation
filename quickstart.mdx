title: "Get Started with MesobOrdering in Minutes"

---

This guide walks you through everything you need to do to take your hotel from a fresh MesobOrdering account to a fully operational digital ordering setup. By the end, you'll have your hotel identity configured, your menu published, your team accounts created, and your QR codes ready to place on tables.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In">
    Open your browser and go to [https://mesobordering.com/login](https://mesobordering.com/login). Enter your registered email address and password, then click **Sign In**.

    If you've forgotten your password, click **Forgot Password?** below the sign-in form. You'll receive a password reset link at your registered email address — follow the link to set a new password and log in.

    <Note>
      If your subscription is in a **PENDING** or **SUSPENDED** state, you'll land on an onboarding screen instead of the main dashboard. Follow the on-screen prompts to resolve your subscription status before continuing.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Your Hotel Identity">
    Once you're logged in, navigate to **System Config** (see [System Configuration](/system-config)) using the left sidebar. This is where you set up how your hotel appears to guests and staff across the platform.

    Fill in the following fields and click **Save** when you're done:

    - **Hotel Name** — your property's display name, shown on the guest menu and receipts.
    - **Brand Color** — the primary color used throughout your guest-facing menu interface.
    - **Logo** — upload your hotel's logo (recommended: PNG with transparent background).
    - **Wi-Fi SSID** — the name of your hotel's guest Wi-Fi network.
    - **Wi-Fi Password** — the guest Wi-Fi password, displayed on the menu for convenience.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build Your Menu">
    Go to **Menu Manager** (see [Menu Manager](/menu-manager)) in the left sidebar. Your menu is organised into categories (for example, *Starters*, *Mains*, *Cocktails*) with individual items nested inside each one.

    Start by creating at least one category, then add menu items under it. For each item, provide:

    - **Name and description** — help guests understand what they're ordering.
    - **Price** — displayed in your configured currency.
    - **Department** — assign each item to either **Kitchen** or **Bar** so orders are routed to the correct team.

    Repeat this process until your full menu is built out. Changes go live immediately — no publish step required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Your Team">
    Navigate to **Staff Management** (see [Staff Management](/staff-management)). Here you create login accounts for every member of staff who will use MesobOrdering during service.

    Add accounts for each role your operation requires:

    - **Waiters** — for taking and tracking table orders.
    - **Kitchen staff** — for the Kitchen View order queue.
    - **Bar staff** — for the Bar order queue.
    - **Cashiers** — for processing payments and closing tabs.

    Assign each team member a temporary password when creating their account. Advise them to change it on first login.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go Live">
    You're ready to start taking orders. Print or display your QR codes and place them on each guest table. When a guest scans a code, their device opens your digital menu automatically in the browser — no app required.

    You and your team can monitor incoming orders in real time from the [Staff Dashboard](/operations-overview). Orders are automatically routed to the Kitchen View or Bar queue based on the department you assigned to each menu item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Bookmark [mesobordering.com/admin](https://mesobordering.com/admin) for quick daily access to your admin dashboard.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Your full admin panel is always available at [https://mesobordering.com/admin](https://mesobordering.com/admin). All management sections — menus, staff, config, analytics, and more — are accessible from the left sidebar once you're logged in.
</Note>
