The System Configuration page is your central control panel for everything that shapes how MesobOrdering looks, behaves, and communicates on behalf of your hotel. You can reach it at `/admin/config`. The page is organized into four tabs — **Identity**, **Payments**, **Alerts**, and **Profile** — each covering a distinct aspect of your setup. A sticky footer bar containing the **Save** button stays visible no matter how far you scroll, so your changes are always one click away from being applied.

<Warning>
  Changes you make on this page are **not saved automatically**. You must click the **Save** button in the sticky footer before navigating away, or your edits will be lost.
</Warning>

---

## Identity Tab

The Identity tab (`/admin/config → Identity`) controls your hotel's brand presentation and the connectivity details your guests see inside the menu app.

### Brand Identity

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Hotel Name** | The display name used throughout the guest-facing app and printed receipts. |
| **Brand Color** | Pick a color using the visual color picker or type a hex value directly (e.g. `#2C7A4B`). This color tints buttons, accents, and highlights in the guest menu. |
| **Logo** | Paste a publicly accessible image URL or use the upload button to host your logo directly. Recommended size: 256 × 256 px, PNG with transparent background. |
| **Currency** | Fixed to **ETB (Ethiopian Birr)**. All prices across menus, orders, and reports are shown in ETB. |

### Guest Connectivity

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Wi-Fi Name (SSID)** | The name of your hotel's guest Wi-Fi network. |
| **Wi-Fi Password** | The password for that network. |

Both fields are displayed to guests inside the menu app so they can connect without asking staff. Leave them blank if you prefer not to show Wi-Fi details publicly.

## Payments Tab

The Payments tab lets you configure the digital payment methods guests can use to settle their bills. You can add as many providers as your hotel supports.

### Adding a Payment Method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Payment form">
    On the Payments tab, locate the **Add Payment Method** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a provider">
    Choose a provider from the **Type** dropdown. Available options include **TeleBirr**, **CBE**, **Awash Bank**, **Dashen Bank**, and others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Display Label** | The name shown to guests, e.g. "Pay via TeleBirr". |
    | **Account / Phone Number** | The account number or phone number that receives payments. |
    | **Account Holder Name** | The registered name on the account, shown for guest confirmation. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the method">
    Click **"Add [Provider Name]"** (the button label updates to match the selected provider). The new method appears in your list immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  TeleBirr requires a valid Ethiopian mobile number. Accepted formats are **09XXXXXXXX**, **07XXXXXXXX**, or **+2519XXXXXXXX / +2517XXXXXXXX**.
</Note>

### Managing Your Payment Methods

Once added, each payment method appears as a row in the list. You can:

- Click **↑** or **↓** to reorder methods — guests see them in the order you set.
- Click **✕** to remove a method you no longer accept.

<Note>
  If you add no payment methods at all, guests will be prompted to **call the waiter** for payment assistance rather than seeing a payment screen.
</Note>

## Alerts Tab

The Alerts tab controls the in-browser audio cues that notify your staff of activity, and connects MesobOrdering to a Telegram bot for real-time notifications.

### Sound Alert Toggles

Use the toggle switches to enable or disable each alert sound:

| Alert | Triggers when… |
|---|---|
| **New Order Entry** | A guest submits a new order from any table. |
| **Kitchen Ready Alert** | The kitchen marks an order as ready for service. |
| **Billing Assistance Request** | A guest requests the bill from their table. |

### Telegram Bot Integration

Connecting a Telegram bot lets you and your team receive live order alerts, bill notifications, and daily summary reports directly in Telegram — even when you're away from the admin dashboard. Once connected, you can also send commands to the bot:

| Command | Action |
|---|---|
| `/status` | Returns the current number of open orders and active tables. |
| `/orders` | Lists all pending orders with table numbers. |
| `/pause [item]` | Temporarily marks a menu item as unavailable. |

#### How to Connect Telegram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the linking process">
    On the Alerts tab, click **"Connect Telegram"**. A 6-digit link code appears on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note your code">
    Keep this page open and take note of the 6-digit code displayed. You'll need it in the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Telegram and find the bot">
    On your phone or desktop, open Telegram and search for **@AlphaBotServiceBot**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link command">
    Send the following message to the bot: `/link [YOUR CODE]` — replacing `[YOUR CODE]` with the 6-digit code. Use the **Copy** button next to the code to copy the full command to your clipboard, then paste it into the Telegram chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the connection">
    Return to the Alerts tab in MesobOrdering and click **"Check Status"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm success">
    Once linked, the Telegram section turns green and displays **"Telegram is connected"**. Your bot will now forward notifications to the linked chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To disconnect the bot at any time, click the **Disconnect** button and confirm the prompt.

<Note>
  Link codes **expire after 10 minutes**. If yours expires before you complete the steps, click **"New Code"** to generate a fresh one and start from Step 2.
</Note>

## Profile Tab

The Profile tab defines your hotel's public-facing listing — what guests see when they scan your QR code and view your hotel's details page.

### Public Profile

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Tagline** | A short, punchy line that sums up your hotel (max 120 characters). Shown prominently on your public profile. |
| **Description** | A longer paragraph introducing your hotel, its atmosphere, and what makes it special. |
| **Website URL** | Your hotel's official website, linked from the profile page. |
| **Cover Image** | The hero image displayed at the top of your public profile. Use a high-resolution landscape image (recommended: 1200 × 400 px). |

### Contact Information

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Phone** | Primary front-desk or reservations number. |
| **Email** | Guest-facing contact email address. |
| **Address** | Your hotel's full street address. |

### Location & Map

You can pin your hotel on the map using any of three methods:

- **Auto-Detect GPS** — Click the **Detect** button to use your browser's location to set the coordinates automatically.
- **Manual Coordinates** — Type your **Latitude** and **Longitude** values, then click **"Generate Map from Coordinates"** to plot the pin.
- **Address to Map** — Type your address into the address field and click the **→** button to geocode it into map coordinates.

Alternatively, paste a **Google Maps embed URL** directly into the embed field to use a custom map view.

### Social Media

Link your hotel's social accounts so guests can follow you. Supported platforms:

**Facebook · Instagram · X/Twitter · TripAdvisor**

Paste the full profile URL for each platform you want to display.

### Amenities

Click any chip to add it to your hotel's amenity list:

**Free Wi-Fi · Parking · Pool · Gym · Spa · Restaurant · Bar · Room Service · Conference Room · Airport Shuttle · Laundry · 24h Reception · Air Conditioning · Elevator · Rooftop Terrace · Kids Area**

To add an amenity that isn't in the preset list, type it into the custom amenity field and click **+ Add**.

### Opening Hours

The opening hours table lets you set your hotel's hours on a per-day basis:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Day** | Day of the week (Monday through Sunday). |
| **Opens** | Time picker for when your hotel opens that day. |
| **Closes** | Time picker for when your hotel closes. |
| **Closed** | Toggle this on for days your hotel is fully closed. |

### Photo Gallery

Add images to your public photo gallery by pasting image URLs into the gallery input and pressing Enter. Each thumbnail shows an **×** button — click it to remove that photo from the gallery.

## Saving Your Changes

When you're done making changes on any tab, click the **Save** button in the sticky footer bar at the bottom of the page. You'll see a **"Saving…"** spinner while the request processes, followed by a **"✓ Settings saved!"** confirmation message once your changes have been successfully stored.
