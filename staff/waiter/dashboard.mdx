**URL:** `/waiter/dashboard` **Access:** After login, or click **Dashboard** in the waiter navigation.

The Waiter Dashboard is your home screen — a quick overview of your current shift.

## Layout

The Waiter layout has a **bottom navigation bar** (mobile) or a **left sidebar** (desktop) with these links:

- **Dashboard** — Home overview
- **Tables** — View and manage tables
- **Orders** — View all orders
- **Place Order** — Take a new order for a table

## Overview cards

**Date & Time display:** Current date and time shown prominently in the top-right corner.

**Stats cards:**

| Card | Icon | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Active Tables** | 🪑 | Number of tables with an active guest session |
| **Pending Orders** | ⏳ | Orders waiting to be prepared (PENDING or PREPARING) |
| **Today's Revenue** | 💰 | Total ETB value from completed orders today |
| **Avg Order Value** | 📊 | Average ETB per order today |

## Waiter call alerts

If a guest at a table presses the **Call Waiter** button on their phone, a **yellow alert banner** instantly appears at the top of your dashboard:

> 🛎️ **Table \[X\]** is calling for a waiter — "I need assistance" _\[Time of request\]_

- Click the **✕** button on the right to dismiss the alert once you've attended to the guest.
- Multiple alerts can stack if several tables call at once.

**Bill Overdue Alert:** If a table has been waiting too long for their bill, a **red alert** appears:

> ⏰ **Overdue Bill — Table \[X\]** — waiting **\[N\] minutes**. Process payment immediately.

Click **Go to Bills →** to jump directly to the bills page.

## Quick actions

Four large buttons for fast navigation:

- **👨‍🍳 Kitchen View** — See the kitchen order queue (with pending orders badge)
- **🪑 Tables** — View all active tables
- **📝 All Orders** — See all recent orders
- **💰 Process Bills** — Jump to bill processing

## Active Tables panel

The left panel shows all currently occupied tables:

- **Table Number** (bold)
- **Guest count** (e.g. "3 guests")
- **Current Total** (in ETB)
- **ACTIVE** badge
- **View** button — Go to the table's detail view
- **Bill** button — Jump directly to billing for that table

## Recent Orders panel

The right panel shows the last 10 orders with columns:

- **Order ID** (short)
- **Table Number**
- **Amount** (ETB)
- **Status** badge (color-coded)
- **View** button — See order details
- **Start Prep** button — Appears for PENDING orders; click to move to PREPARING
