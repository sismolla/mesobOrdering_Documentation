MesobOrdering is an enterprise QR-code-driven hotel ordering, POS, and inventory management platform. Guests scan a QR code at their table to browse your digital menu, place orders, and request bills — while management controls operations, inventory ledgers, and staff from a powerful admin dashboard.

## How MesobOrdering Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your hotel profile">
    Log in to `mesobordering.com/admin` and configure your brand identity, payment methods, Wi-Fi credentials, and Telegram alerts in [System Configuration](/system-config).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure master inventory data">
    Set up storage locations (Central Store, Kitchen, Bar), unit categories, packaging rules, and suppliers in [Inventory Overview](/inventory-overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your menu & recipes">
    Use the [Menu Manager](/menu-manager) to add menu items and map recipes in [Recipes & Servings](/inventory-recipes-projections) for automatic stock auto-deduction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    Create accounts for storekeepers, head chefs, waiters, kitchen, bar, and cashiers in [Staff Management](/staff-management).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live & monitor shifts">
    Place QR codes on tables. Monitor real-time activity from the [Staff Dashboard](/operations-overview) and close cashier shifts with cash float reconciliation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your admin panel is at [mesobordering.com/admin](https://mesobordering.com/admin). Bookmark it for quick access.
</Note>
