MesobOrdering is a QR-code-driven hotel ordering and management platform. Guests scan a QR code at their table to browse your digital menu, place orders, and request bills — while you manage everything from a powerful admin dashboard. This documentation covers everything you need to configure and operate your hotel's MesobOrdering account.

## How MesobOrdering Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your hotel profile">
    Log in to `mesobordering.com/admin` and configure your brand identity, payment methods, Wi-Fi credentials, and public profile in [System Configuration](/system-config).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your menu">
    Use the [Menu Manager](/menu-manager) to create categories and add all your food and drink items — with prices, descriptions, dietary tags, and prep times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    Create accounts for your waiters, kitchen staff, bar staff, and cashiers in [Staff Management](/staff-management), each with the right role and permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Place QR codes on tables. Guests scan and start ordering. Monitor real-time activity from the [Staff Dashboard](/operations-overview) and [Kitchen View](/kitchen-view).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your admin panel is at [mesobordering.com/admin](https://mesobordering.com/admin). Bookmark it for quick access.
</Note>
